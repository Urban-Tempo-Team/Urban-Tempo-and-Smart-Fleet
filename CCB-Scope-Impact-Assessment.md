Project: Urban Tempo Smart Fleet & Transit
Requested Change: The Transit Director wants to add a feature that lets passengers see how crowded buses are through the transit app.
Impact on Project
Scope: We would need to add a new feature to track how many people are on each bus.
Schedule: This would take extra time to develop and test.
Cost: We may need to spend more money on cameras or passenger counters.
Risks: We would need to consider passenger privacy and make sure the tracking system works correctly.
Team Recommendation: 
Our team thinks this would be a useful feature, but we recommend holding off on it for now. Adding it would take more time and money than we originally planned. We think it makes more sense to finish what we already have planned and look into adding this feature later.
