# Project Epics

## Maintenance
This epic includes issues related to scheduling, tracking, and completing maintenance on fleet vehicles.

## Routing
This epic includes issues related to bus routes, delays, detours, and vehicle availability.

## Training
This epic includes issues related to helping users understand how to use the fleet management system.

## Engine Diagnostics
This epic includes issues related to monitoring vehicle health, detecting engine problems, and predicting possible breakdowns.
